FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Delivery time varies depending on complexity and production capacity. Please contact us for a specific estimate for your order.
Do you manufacture from custom drawings?
Yes. We develop and manufacture custom solutions to your technical drawings or specifications — from individual prototypes to series production.
Which standards and norms are met?
Our products meet the common standards: AN/MS (US Military), JIC 37° Flare, BSP, DIN, NPT. Quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015.
Is there a product warranty?
Yes. All supAero products carry a comprehensive quality warranty. In case of material or manufacturing defects, we replace products at no charge.
