About supAero

Superb Hydraulics, Engineered for Performance

Founded by engineers with motorsport DNA, supAero has delivered precision fluid systems to professionals across Europe and beyond for over 15 years.

Our Story

Founded from a Passion for Engineering

supAero was born from a simple conviction: high-performance hydraulics shouldn't be reserved for Formula 1 teams alone. We bring professional AN fittings, hoses and connectors directly to motorsport teams, aerospace suppliers, UAV developers and industrial customers.

Our products are developed and tested to the most stringent quality standards. Every fitting, every hose — designed to perform where failure is not an option.

Today we operate in over 50 countries, supplying customers from club sport level to world championships.
